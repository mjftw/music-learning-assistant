The drone sheet, opened from 3a's ▼
The note comes from the circle, so the sheet only covers what the circle can't: the sound. The octave is on the pill's − / +. On/off sits in the header.
4a
Sound only
Always the key's tonic. The octave is set only with the pill's − / + (any octave on a full piano, A0–C8, starting nearest the middle of the instrument's range), so the sheet holds just on/off and the sound: pure, warm or reed.
The pill from 2b, moved onto the circle
The drone follows the key, so it lives on the circle rather than with the scale.
3a
In the centre disc
Above the key signature, mirroring the ♯/♭ toggle below it. Reads as "this key, sounding".
3b
In the circle's corner
In the empty corner of the circle's square: part of the circle area but clear of the wedges and key signature.
Drone without a new element
Three ways to fit the drone into the existing screen. All are live: tap to toggle, tap a note on the stave to hear it. Scale and drone never sound together.
2a
Transport mode: scale | drone
A switch in the traversal row turns the transport into the drone's controls. ▶ holds the drone, the tempo stepper steps the note, and "tonic" opens the drone sheet. They can't sound together, so it's one control either way.
2b
Drone pill beside the scale
A second pill in the same row as SCALE, under the key name. Tap to hold the tonic; ▼ opens the drone sheet. It sits with the key it follows.
2c
Tap the selected key again
Tapping the key you're on holds its tonic. The wedge gets an inner outline, and the panel footer switches to a drone readout (edit › opens the sheet). No extra chrome, but hard to discover.
